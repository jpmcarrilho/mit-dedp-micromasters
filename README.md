# MIT DEDP Micromasters

This repository contains my R code and assignments from the MIT Data, Economics, and Development Policy (DEDP) Micromasters program.

## Courses

### 1. Data Analysis for Social Scientists
Code, exploratory data analysis, and regression modeling performed throughout the course modules. Topics range from basic statistics to randomized controlled trials and causal inference techniques.

**Course Modules & Topics:**
- **Module 1**: Introduction to the Course *(Incomplete)*
- **Module 2**: Fundamentals of Probability, Random Variables, Joint Distributions & Collecting Data *(Completed)*
  - Script: `02-probability-and-random-variables.R`
- **Module 3**: Describing Data, Joint and Conditional Distributions of Random Variables *(Incomplete)*
  - Script: `03-describing-data-and-distributions.R`
- **Module 4**: Functions and Moments of a Random Variable & Intro to Regressions *(Incomplete)*
- **Module 5**: Special Distributions, the Sample Mean, the Central Limit Theorem *(Completed)*
- **Module 6**: Assessing and Deriving Estimators, Confidence Intervals, and Hypothesis Testing *(Incomplete)*
  - Script: `06-estimators-and-hypothesis-testing.R`
- **Module 7**: Causality, Analyzing Randomized Experiments, & Nonparametric Regression *(Incomplete)*
  - Script: `07-causality-and-randomized-experiments.R`
- **Module 8**: Single and Multivariate Linear Models *(Incomplete)*
  - Script: `08-linear-models.R`
- **Module 9**: Practical Issues in Running Regressions and Omitted Variable Bias *(Completed)*
  - Script: `09-regressions-and-ovb.R`
- **Module 10**: Endogeneity, Instrumental Variables, Experimental Design, and Data Visualization *(Included in Mod 11 script)*
- **Module 11**: Endogeneity and Instrumental Variables
  - Script: `11-endogeneity-and-instrumental-variables.R`

### 2. Political Economy and Economic Development
Analyses surrounding institutions, corruption, and the political drivers of economic outcomes in developing nations.
